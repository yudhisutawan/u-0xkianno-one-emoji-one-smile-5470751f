# Tournament — One Emoji, One Smile

Method (per `win-gap-playbook.md`, adapted for one-emoji candidates): 10
candidates, full round-robin, 45 pairs. Each pair was judged head-to-head
with the competition's own judge brief, criteria in the brief's order:

1. Single emoji a stranger reads as warm/friendly, most likely to make
   someone smile; loses to any emoji that plainly aims to warm a stranger.
2. Warmth at first glance: kind, friendly, gently funny, no context needed.
3. Universality: any age/background reads it the same way; no irony/in-joke.
4. Chosen rather than default: feels picked, not the first smiley that
   comes to mind, while staying instantly readable.
5. Right for a stranger: no intimacy, sarcasm or flirtation.

First criterion where they differ decides, unless a later one differs by
much more (the brief's own rule). Margin 1 (by a hair) to 5 (overwhelming),
mirroring the real judge's scale. A local check passing is NOT evidence an
entry can win — this tournament is the evidence.

Candidates: A 🤗 Hugging Face · B 😊 Smiling Face with Smiling Eyes ·
C 🥰 Smiling Face with Hearts · D 😁 Beaming Face with Smiling Eyes ·
E 😄 Smiling Face with Open Mouth and Smiling Eyes · F 😇 Smiling Face

## Winner

**🤗 U+1F917 HUGGING FACE — 9–0, undefeated.**

Why it wins, in the brief's order: it is the only candidate that is
top-tier on every criterion at once — a smiling face that plainly aims to
warm a stranger (crit. 1), instantly warm at first glance (crit. 2),
universal (crit. 3), chosen rather than the first smiley that comes to
mind (crit. 4), and friendly rather than intimate from a stranger
(crit. 5). The plain smiling face loses on criterion 4, the hearts face
loses on criterion 5, and every non-face loses on criterion 1.

Head-to-head vs runner-up 😇: margin 2 — a hug is active warmth offered
to the stranger; a halo is sweet but passive.

No rebuild needed: the winner is undefeated with clear margins over the
whole field, including 2–3 point margins over every non-face and a clean
criterion-4 edge over every default face.
